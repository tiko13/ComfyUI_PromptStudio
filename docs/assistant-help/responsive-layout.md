Layout follows width, rotation and split view, including wide touch screens. Image's Chats and phone Controls open drawers. Close, Escape, the dimmed background or swiping the heading toward its screen edge dismisses them. Drawers isolate background controls and return focus to their opener.
Image's phone composer has Options for generation toggles, Create/Edit and Constraints. Tablet landscape uses one text row with Send and More, plus a small status line. More opens upward with those options, Undo, Stop, Reroll and run details. Longer drafts expand while editing. Rotation and collapse preserve settings and drafts. Tablets keep Controls beside the conversation.
Video keeps the workflow selector visible. Tools has workflow maintenance, system status, New seed, Prompt, Duplicate and Reset; Escape/outside taps close it. Projects opens history; Shots opens the phone inspector. Tablets keep Shots alongside the workspace. Expand LoRAs & reference adapters to edit. Generate stays at the bottom.
Image cards have Edit image, Details for saved inputs, and More for Caption, Upscale, Copy to new chat, Send to Video, XYZ and Delete message. Actions use the selected card; deleting its entire message requires confirmation.
Settings > Image scale also resizes touch thumbnails. Tiny cards put Edit image and Details in More; full-size previews stay unchanged.
Threads and Video Director open at the end. Images load near the viewport; scrolling up loads older ones and stops following new image loads.
In previews, pinch/double-tap to zoom, then drag or use arrows to pan. Swipe down at fit size to close, never delete; Close and Zoom/Fit are alternatives. Image sheets dismiss by dragging their header down.
Touch controls have larger targets. Browser zoom works. Supported Reduced Motion, reduced transparency and increased contrast preferences simplify animation and surfaces.
