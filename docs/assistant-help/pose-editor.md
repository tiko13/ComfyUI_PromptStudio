In References on a compatible Structure guide workflow, Add pose opens a default skeleton without a photo. For photos, expand the card, select Structure guide and Pose, then Extract & edit pose. Extraction queues the installed pose preprocessor. A PNG alone has no editable joints.
Drag joints freely, or select Person, Edit part and Joint. A rotatable joint shows a ring: drag it to rotate the attached limb around the fixed joint, preserving bone lengths. Shift snaps to 15°. Wrists/fingers need attached points; tips and face points have no ring. Drag the center to move freely. Move person carries all parts; Move part adjusts body, hand or face. Moving a body wrist carries its fingers.
Scroll to zoom at the pointer; Zoom keeps the view center. Hold middle mouse and drag to pan, or choose Pan for left-button dragging. Release to stop. Fit view recenters. Photo opacity changes only the background.
Hide joint removes connected lines; Place joint restores it at a canvas click. Add hand builds fingers at a wrist. Add/remove people, Mirror person and Rotate / resize adjust composition; Body transforms the whole person. Undo/Redo and Reset pose affect the draft. Arrows nudge joints; Shift increases the step.
Apply pose saves the guide and joints without generating. Cancel or Escape discards edits. Reopen with Edit pose. Saved generations retain pose data for replay. Extraction/upload errors keep the existing reference; uploads can be retried.
Generation uses the saved guide without re-extracting. Edit prompt analysis uses saved joint geometry, retaining sideways leg movement even at shallow angles. Older pose instructions refresh on the next edit. Strength, framing and target placement still apply; extraction crops are baked in. Replace the image to start over. Reference + structure keeps the photo for appearance. A photo-free pose is structure-only. Video's ordinary image inputs do not support pose ControlNet.
